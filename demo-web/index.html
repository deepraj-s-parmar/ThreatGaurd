Digital Arrest Scams

Common approach

A caller impersonates a law-enforcement or government official and pressures you with claims of an investigation or so-called digital arrest.

Warning signs

  • Makes alarming claims about alleged illegal activity
  • Pressures you to remain on a video call
  • Demands money to supposedly resolve the situation
  • Orders you to keep the conversation secret

Avoid doing this

  • Never transfer money under pressure
  • Do not share identity documents or financial details
  • End the call and verify independently

Safer next steps

  • End the call and contact the agency using an official number
  • If money is lost, call India's cybercrime helpline at 1930 promptly
  • Submit a report at the official cybercrime portal
💳

UPI / Payment Scams

Common approach

A person claims you are receiving a refund or payment and asks you to approve a collect request or scan a QR code.

Warning signs

  • Unexpected payment requests from unknown people
  • Claims that scanning a QR code is required to receive money
  • Requests for your UPI PIN or payment credentials

Avoid doing this

  • Never enter your UPI PIN to receive money
  • Avoid scanning payment QR codes from untrusted sources

Safer next steps

  • Decline unexpected payment requests
  • Check transactions only through your bank's official app
🏦

Fake Bank Support

Common approach

A message or caller impersonates a bank and threatens account restrictions unless you urgently complete a KYC update.

Warning signs

  • Threats of immediate account suspension
  • Urgent KYC links sent through unofficial channels
  • Requests for OTPs, PINs, CVV or card information

Avoid doing this

  • Never disclose OTPs, CVV numbers or PINs
  • Do not open unexpected links

Safer next steps

  • Contact your bank using the number on its official website or card
  • Visit an official branch if you need further assistance
🔗

Phishing

Common approach

A message directs you to a lookalike website designed to imitate a trusted bank, service or organisation.

Warning signs

  • Lookalike or misspelled website addresses
  • Unexpected or insecure website connections
  • Pressure to click or act immediately

Avoid doing this

  • Do not follow links in unexpected messages
  • Never enter passwords or payment details on unverified websites

Safer next steps

  • Open the official website yourself instead of using the message link
  • Use multi-factor authentication where available
📈

Investment Scams

Common approach

An offer promises unusually high or guaranteed returns and claims that an investment has little or no risk.

Warning signs

  • "Guaranteed returns" or "100% profit"
  • Claims that an investment has no risk
  • Pressure to send money quickly

Avoid doing this

  • Do not invest based on unsolicited messages or online tips
  • Be cautious of unverified investment groups

Safer next steps

  • Check intermediaries using SEBI's official resources
  • Seek advice from a qualified, independent financial professional
💼

Job / Task Scams

Common approach

A recruiter offers easy online work but asks for a joining, training or registration payment before you can begin.

Warning signs

  • Upfront payment requested to access the job
  • Unusually high payment for simple tasks
  • Recruitment handled only through informal messaging groups

Avoid doing this

  • Do not pay to secure a job or unlock tasks
  • Do not share banking passwords, PINs or OTPs

Safer next steps

  • Verify vacancies through the organisation's official website
  • Report suspected online fraud through the official cybercrime portal
